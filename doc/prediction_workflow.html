4 Feature Engine & Regime Detection

Python Engine: scripts/predict_weighted_analysis.py

Transforms raw JSON into a 109+ column feature tensor, incorporating advanced features:

  • Lag Features: Explicit past price and volume data (e.g., Close_lag_5).
  • Short-term Momentum: Rate of Change (ROC) and returns over short periods (e.g., ROC_5d, Return_1d).
  • Trend Context: Indicators like price relative to rolling high/low, EMA slopes (e.g., PriceToHigh_20d, EMA_Slope_10).
  • Rolling Statistics: Mean, Standard Deviation, Skewness, and Sharpe Ratio over rolling windows (e.g., RollingMean_20d).
  • Macroeconomic Data: Detailed indicators including World Bank GDP, Inflation, and Consumption growth.
  • Credit Risk & Dollar: Features derived from HYG/LQD spreads and DXY index.
  • Commodity & Volatility: Incorporates commodity returns, betas, and VIX-based volatility proxies.
  • Options Market Data: Implied Volatility (IV), IV Rank, and Put/Call Ratio.
  • Earnings & Fundamentals: Surprise data, short interest, and traditional valuation metrics.

Market Regime Analysis

Utilizes a Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) or KMeans clustering on a vector of market indicators (e.g., VIX, credit spreads, relative strength, interest rates, World Bank macro data) to identify the current market state (e.g., Risk-On, Risk-Off, High Volatility).

Introduces regime-specific features, probabilities, and interaction terms (e.g., Regime_x_PC_Ratio, Regime_x_HYG_LQD) to contextualize predictions.

Algorithm Flowchart

JSON Input → 109+ Feature Engineering (incl. Lag, ROC, Rolling Stats, Macro, Credit, Commodities, Regimes) → MLP Training (Adam Optimizer) → Dual Path Inference (Deterministic + Monte Carlo Dropout 12x with per-feature noise scaling) → Heuristic Weighting (incl. Consumer Multiplier, Analyst/Technical Signals, Regime Context) → JSON Output (1-day, 1-month, 6-month, 1-year predictions)

5 MLP Training & MC Inference

Model Architecture: 256 → 128 → 64 → 32 (ReLU)

Trains a fresh MLP model on historical sequences (SEQ_LEN=45). 12x Monte Carlo Dropout passes generate statistical spreads for confidence scoring. MC noise is scaled per-feature based on recent volatility for more robust interval generation.

6 Final Signal Weighting & Confidence

Logic: Heuristic Adjustments & Confidence Scoring

Neural network output is adjusted by real-world signals and confidence metrics:

  • Consumption Multiplier: Applied to consumer sectors based on World Bank consumption growth trends (±3%).
  • Technical/Analyst Influence: Integrates external technical scores, analyst consensus (e.g., Buy/Hold/Sell), and numerical analyst target premiums.
  • Market Regime Context: Predictions are nudged based on the identified market regime and its interaction with other signals.
  • Global Health Score: Dampens predictions if broader global macro indicators fall below a threshold.
  • Confidence Scoring: Confidence in predictions (1-day, 1-month, 6-month, 1-year) is calculated based on historical accuracy (CV-MAPE), data completeness, history depth, and analyst coverage.

7 Persistence Layer

Backend Utility: POST /api/prediction/save

Saves 1-day, 1-month, 6-month, and 1-year price targets, along with trajectory data and confidence scores, to user_stock_predictions. Stored data includes macro_features_used and regime information for model observability.
