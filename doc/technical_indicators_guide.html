Technical Indicators & Signals

This guide explains how the "Trading Signal" is calculated on the Stock Search page. The total score is a weighted sum of technical, fundamental, and sentiment metrics.

Scoring Methodology

The total score typically ranges from -14 to +14. This score is then mapped to a recommendation:

Score Range Signal Color
Score ≥ 8 Strong Buy Green
3 ≤ Score < 8 Buy Light Green
-2 < Score < 3 Hold Gray
-7 < Score ≤ -2 Sell Orange
Score ≤ -7 Strong Sell Red

Moving Averages (SMA)

SMA 20/50 Crossover +2 / -2

Measures the relationship between short-term and medium-term price trends.

SMA(n) = (Price_1 + Price_2 + ... + Price_n) / n
  • Bullish (+2): Current Price > SMA20 AND SMA20 > SMA50.
  • Bearish (-2): Current Price < SMA20 AND SMA20 < SMA50.

Relative Strength Index (RSI)

RSI (14-Day) +2 / -2

A momentum oscillator that measures the speed and change of price movements.

RSI = 100 - [100 / (1 + (Avg Gain / Avg Loss))]
  • Oversold (+2): RSI < 30 (Suggests the stock is undervalued or due for a bounce).
  • Overbought (-2): RSI > 70 (Suggests the stock is overextended or due for a pullback).
  • Neutral (0): RSI between 30 and 70.

Momentum & Trends

Price Momentum +2 / -2

Calculates the rate of change in price over the last 10 trading days.

Momentum = (Current Price / Price 10 Days Ago) - 1
  • Positive (+2): Momentum > 2% (Recent strong upward move).
  • Negative (-2): Momentum < -2% (Recent strong downward move).

Volumetric Trend +1 / -1

Compares current volume to the 3-month average to confirm trend strength.

  • Bullish Confirmation (+1): Price is UP and Volume > 1.2x Average.
  • Bearish Confirmation (-1): Price is DOWN and Volume > 1.2x Average.

Fundamental Valuation

P/E Ratio Relative +2 / -2

Evaluates the stock's valuation relative to its 5-year historical average.

  • Value (+2): Current P/E < 80% of 5yr Mean.
  • Expensive (-2): Current P/E > 120% of 5yr Mean.

Market Cap Multiplier +1 / 0

Small and Mid-cap stocks receive a minor volatility bonus if trending positively.

  • Growth Bonus (+1): Market Cap < $10B AND Trend is Bullish.

Sentiment Analysis

News Sentiment +2 / -2

Analyzes the tone of the latest headlines and descriptions using an AI-enhanced financial lexicon. The raw analysis is normalized to a -1.0 (extremely bearish) to +1.0 (extremely bullish) scale.

Scoring Logic:

  • Bullish News (+2): Sentiment Score > 0.1.
  • Bearish News (-2): Sentiment Score < -0.1.
  • Neutral (0): Score between -0.1 and 0.1.

Note: This analyzes both the title and description of the top 10 most relevant news articles for the stock.

ETF GPS Scoring Methodology

Unlike individual stocks, ETFs are scored using a Growth, Performance, and Sentiment (GPS) model tuned for fund-level characteristics. The total score is out of 100.

Core Score Components (Weights)

  • 52-Week Price Return (25.5%): Rewards long-term upward momentum.
  • Thematic News Signal (21.25%): Measures overlap between ETF holdings and trending news tickers.
  • 3-Month Momentum (17%): Rewards short-term price strength.
  • Macro Tailwind (15%): Structural macro confirmation based on FDI and trade trends.
  • Liquidity Score (12.75%): Based on average daily trading volume.
  • Expense Ratio Efficiency (8.5%): Rewards funds with lower annual management fees.

ETF GPS Bonuses

  • Hot Stock Overlap (+8): Applied if ≥ 3 holdings are in the current Hot Stocks list.
  • Trending Theme (+5): Applied if the ETF theme matches a trending AI/Tech sub-sector.
  • Tech Adoption Validation (Up to +6): Bonus points for themes like AI/Semis based on internet penetration and ICT exports.
  • High Momentum (+4): Applied if 52-week return ≥ 25%.
  • Under-the-Radar (+3): Rewards discovery of smaller thematic funds (AUM < $500M).

Minimum Qualifying Score: 55 / 100
