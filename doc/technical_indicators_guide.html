Technical Indicators & Signals

This guide explains how the "Trading Signal" and "GPS Score" are calculated. These metrics combine technical, fundamental, and AI-driven sentiment analysis to evaluate investment opportunities.

Scoring Methodology

The total score typically ranges from -14 to +14. This score is then mapped to a recommendation:

Score Range Signal Color
Score ≥ 5 BUY Green
-4 ≤ Score ≤ 4 HOLD Gray
Score ≤ -5 SELL Red

Moving Averages (SMA)

SMA 20/50 Crossover +3 / -3

Measures the relationship between short-term and medium-term price trends.

SMA(n) = (Price_1 + Price_2 + ... + Price_n) / n
  • Bullish (+3): SMA20 > SMA50.
  • Bearish (-3): SMA20 < SMA50.

Relative Strength Index (RSI)

RSI (14-Day) +2 / -2

A momentum oscillator that measures the speed and change of price movements.

RSI = 100 - [100 / (1 + (Avg Gain / Avg Loss))]
  • Oversold (+2): RSI < 30.
  • Overbought (-2): RSI > 70.

Momentum & Trends

Price Momentum +2 / -2

Calculates whether the price is higher or lower than 10 periods ago.

Momentum = Current Price - Price 10 Days Ago
  • Positive (+2): Momentum > 0.
  • Negative (-2): Momentum < 0.

Price vs SMA50 +1 / -1

Confirms if the current price is above its 50-day average trend.

  • Bullish (+1): Price > SMA50.
  • Bearish (-1): Price < SMA50.

Fundamental Valuation

P/E Ratio +1 / -1

Evaluates the stock's valuation based on earnings.

  • Value (+1): P/E < 15.
  • Expensive (-1): P/E > 25.

P/B Ratio +1 / -1

Evaluates the stock's valuation relative to its book value.

  • Value (+1): P/B < 2.
  • Expensive (-1): P/B > 5.

Market Cap +1 / -1

Rewards large-cap stability and identifies small-cap risk.

  • Large Cap (+1): Market Cap > $200B.
  • Small Cap (-1): Market Cap < $2B.

Sentiment Analysis

News Sentiment +2 / -2

Analyzes the tone of the latest headlines using a financial lexicon.

  • Bullish News (+2): Sentiment Score > 0.1.
  • Bearish News (-2): Sentiment Score < -0.1.

Volatility Rating +1 / -1

Adjusts score based on historical annualized price volatility.

  • Low Volatility (+1): Increased stability.
  • High Volatility (-1): Increased risk.

DeepMoney GPS Score (Individual Stocks)

The GPS Score (Global Performance Score) is a proprietary metric used in DeepMoney analysis to evaluate a stock's total potential. It is calculated on a scale of 0 to 100 based on fundamental growth and AI price forecasting.

* These calculation metrics are applied identically in both the deepmoney_sync discovery engine and the update_predictions portfolio maintenance script.

1. Core Components Up to 19 pts each

The base score is built from six specific metrics (five at 19 pts, one at 5 pts), each scaled against a target benchmark:

  • Analyst Upside (19 pts): Gap between current price and mean analyst target.
    • Benchmark: 30% upside = 19 pts.
  • Revenue Growth (19 pts): Year-over-year revenue growth.
    • Benchmark: 30% growth = 19 pts.
  • Earnings Growth (19 pts): Year-over-year earnings growth.
    • Benchmark: 25% growth = 19 pts.
  • 52-Week Price Change (19 pts): Overall price performance over the last year.
    • Benchmark: 20% gain = 19 pts.
  • MLP Predicted Upside (19 pts): AI-predicted 1-month price movement.
    • Benchmark: 3% growth = 19 pts (Configurable via GPS_PREDICTION_MAX).

2. Confidence Component Up to 5 pts

  • MLP Confidence Score: Scaling of the AI model's confidence in its forecast.
    • Benchmark: 100 confidence = 5 pts.

3. Final Result & Thresholds

  • Cap: The final score is capped at 100.0.
  • Selection Criteria: In the automated sync process, stocks are typically only recommended if they pass the ML Validation Gate (predicted change >= 1.5%). Recommendation buckets apply a GPS threshold (typically > 10.0) for discovery surfacing.

Summary: A high GPS score indicates a stock with strong analyst backing, high revenue/earnings growth, positive momentum, and a favorable high-confidence AI prediction.

ETF GPS Scoring Methodology

Unlike individual stocks, ETFs are scored using a Growth, Performance, and Sentiment (GPS) model tuned for fund-level characteristics. The total score is out of 100.

Core Score Components (Weights)

  • 52-Week Price Return (25.5%): Rewards long-term upward momentum.
  • Thematic News Signal (21.25%): Measures overlap between ETF holdings and trending news tickers.
  • 3-Month Momentum (17%): Rewards short-term price strength.
  • Macro Tailwind (15%): Structural macro confirmation based on FDI and trade trends.
  • Liquidity Score (12.75%): Based on average daily trading volume.
  • Expense Ratio Efficiency (8.5%): Rewards funds with lower annual management fees.

ETF GPS Bonuses

  • Hot Stock Overlap (+8): Applied if ≥ 3 holdings are in the current Hot Stocks list.
  • Trending Theme (+5): Applied if the ETF theme matches a trending AI/Tech sub-sector.
  • Tech Adoption Validation (Up to +6): Bonus points for themes like AI/Semis based on internet penetration and ICT exports.
  • High Momentum (+4): Applied if 52-week return ≥ 25%.
  • Under-the-Radar (+3): Rewards discovery of smaller thematic funds (AUM < $500M).

Minimum Qualifying Score: 55 / 100
