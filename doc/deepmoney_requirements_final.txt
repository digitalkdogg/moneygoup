Version 2.1 | March 2026 | Includes Hot ETF Discovery Module

  Revision Note

  This document supersedes both the initial v2.0 draft and the first
  AI/Tech revision. All original requirements are retained. Version 2.1
  introduces a new Hot ETF Discovery Module (Section 9) to complement
  the existing stock discovery pipeline. The ETF module identifies
  trending exchange-traded funds priced under $400/share and persists
  discoveries to a dedicated database schema. This revision also adds
  ETF-specific fields to the API response schema and an ETF data source
  table in the external data sources section.

1. Overview

The current DeepMoney API performs basic NLP-based entity extraction and
keyword sentiment analysis on news articles to surface trending stocks
and market sectors. Version 2.0 upgrades it into a Smart Stock Discovery
Engine that identifies stocks with strong 1-year growth potential,
priced under $150/share, with a verifiable track record of growth or
demonstrated stability.

Version 2.1 extends this engine to include a Hot ETF Discovery Module.
This module discovers trending exchange-traded funds priced under
$400/share, scores them using an ETF-specific GPS model, and persists
qualifying ETFs to a dedicated database. The ETF module runs in parallel
with the stock pipeline and surfaces both thematic and sector-based
opportunities investors may be overlooking.

This revision also extends the scope of the existing AI and Technology
Stock Discovery Module, which retains its core design principle: the
engine must surface stocks that investors are unlikely to already know
about.

2. Goals & Success Criteria

2.1 Primary Goals

-   Surface stocks under $150/share with strong 1-year forward growth
    potential.

-   Discover hot ETFs priced under $400/share with momentum and thematic
    relevance.

-   Distinguish true growth stocks from noise by combining news signals
    with fundamental financial data.

-   Identify both established growth stocks and up-and-coming stable
    stocks across all sectors.

-   Discovery principle: Proactively surface emerging AI/Tech stocks not
    yet on most investors' radar — explicitly avoiding over-indexing on
    large-cap names.

-   Reduce false positives through multi-dimensional GPS scoring for
    both stocks and ETFs.

-   Persist all qualifying ETF discoveries to the database for trend
    tracking and historical analysis.

3. Current State & Limitations

The current implementation has the following known limitations that this
upgrade will address:

-   No financial data integration — the API only uses news articles with
    no access to price, revenue, earnings, or valuation data.

-   Weak ticker resolution — organization name matching is fragile; many
    mentioned companies are missed or incorrectly mapped.

-   No price filter — stocks of any price are returned.

-   No growth qualification — no mechanism to determine whether a stock
    has a history of growth.

-   No AI/Tech vertical awareness — the API cannot proactively find
    relevant stocks in fast-moving technology verticals.

-   No ETF discovery — the API is entirely stock-focused and does not
    surface exchange-traded funds.

-   No database persistence — discovered assets are not stored for
    historical analysis or trend tracking.

-   Bias toward large, well-covered companies — stocks with the most
    news coverage score highest, which naturally advantages mega-cap
    names regardless of growth potential.

4. Stock Universe & Growth Classification

All stocks — regardless of how they are discovered — must pass the same
price filter and be classified into one of the following growth profiles
before receiving a GPS score.

4.1 Price Filter (Hard Gate — All Stocks)

-   Any stock at or above $150/share is excluded before scoring.
    Configurable via PRICE_FILTER_MAX env var.

-   Price check occurs immediately after ticker resolution, before any
    enrichment API calls.

4.2 Growth Classifications

Profile A — Established Growth Stock

-   Revenue growth ≥ 10% YoY in at least 2 of the last 3 fiscal years.

-   EPS growth positive in at least 2 of the last 3 fiscal years.

-   52-week price return ≥ 10%.

-   Current price < $150/share.

Profile B — Up-and-Coming Stable Stock

-   Revenue growth ≥ 15% in the most recent fiscal year.

-   Market cap between $300M and $10B (small-to-mid cap).

-   Analyst consensus 1-year price target implies ≥ 15% upside.

-   No more than 2 consecutive quarters of negative gross margin.

-   Current price < $150/share.

Profile C — AI/Tech Hyper-Growth Stock [NEW PROFILE]

A third classification for AI and technology companies that relaxes
profitability requirements in recognition that high-growth tech
companies commonly reinvest aggressively in R&D.

-   Sector must be AI, Semiconductor, Cloud/SaaS, Cybersecurity, or Data
    Infrastructure.

-   Revenue growth ≥ 20% in the most recent fiscal year.

-   Gross margin ≥ 50% (scalable, IP-driven business model).

-   Analyst consensus 1-year price target implies ≥ 20% upside.

-   R&D spend as % of revenue ≥ 10%.

-   Current price < $150/share.

6.3 Discovery Strategy C — Company Feed Deep Scan (Pass 2) [NEW]

For the top trending tickers identified in the initial news pass, the
engine performs a deep scan of the company's individual Yahoo Finance

-   Weighted Sentiment: Co-mentioned stocks receive sentiment scores
    weighted by CO_MENTION_SENTIMENT_WEIGHT (0.5) to reflect the
    indirect nature of the mention.

-   Throughput: Bounded to the top 15 trending tickers per cycle to
    ensure performance.

9. Hot ETF Discovery Module [NEW MODULE]

  This is a new module introduced in v2.1. It runs in parallel with the
  existing stock and AI/Tech discovery pipelines. All ETF discoveries
  that pass scoring thresholds are persisted to the database (see
  Section 9.5).

9.1 Module Overview

The Hot ETF Discovery Module identifies exchange-traded funds with
strong recent price momentum, growing news coverage, and thematic
alignment with currently trending market sectors. ETFs are scored using
an ETF-specific GPS model that weights momentum, liquidity, and thematic
relevance. All qualifying ETFs are stored in a dedicated database table
for historical analysis and trend tracking.

Key design constraints:

-   Price gate: Only ETFs priced under $400/share are considered.

-   Liquidity gate: Only ETFs with average daily volume > 50,000 shares
    are considered.

-   AUM floor: Only ETFs with AUM > $50M to ensure sufficient liquidity
    and viability.

-   No leverage: Leveraged and inverse ETFs are excluded by default
    (configurable via INCLUDE_LEVERAGED_ETFS env var).

9.2 ETF Price Filter (Hard Gate)

-   Any ETF at or above $400/share is excluded before scoring.
    Configurable via ETF_PRICE_FILTER_MAX env var.

-   Price check occurs immediately after ETF ticker resolution, before
    any enrichment calls.

-   The $400 ceiling is intentionally generous relative to the stock
    filter to accommodate the naturally higher per-share prices of many
    broad-market ETFs.

9.3 ETF Discovery Strategies

Strategy A — News Signal Alignment

The AI/Tech keyword taxonomy from Section 6.1 is extended to also tag
ETFs that hold significant exposure to trending sub-sectors. When an
ETF's top holdings overlap with stocks appearing in trending news, the
ETF receives a News Signal Bonus in ETF GPS scoring.

-   ETFs are matched to news signals via their top-10 holdings list
    (sourced from Yahoo Finance ETF profile).

-   Overlap threshold: ≥ 2 of the ETF's top-10 holdings must appear in
    the current cycle's trending tickers.

-   Signal bonus applied per matching trending holding (capped at +10
    pts).

Strategy B — ETF Dynamic Screener

Yahoo Finance ETF screener presets (e.g., most_actives, day_gainers) are
queried each cycle and post-filtered using the ETF filter chain below.

9.5.3 Persistence Logic

The ETF persistence workflow follows these steps each discovery cycle:

-   Step 1 — Discover: Run all three ETF discovery strategies (Sections
    9.3 A–C) and consolidate candidates.

-   Step 2 — Filter: Apply the ETF filter chain (Section 9.3 B). Discard
    non-qualifying ETFs.

-   Step 3 — Score: Compute ETF GPS score for each remaining candidate.

-   Step 4 — Threshold: Discard ETFs with ETF GPS score < 55.

-   Step 5 — Upsert: For each qualifying ETF, perform an upsert into
    hot_etfs keyed on (ticker, snapshot_date). This prevents duplicate
    rows if the cycle is re-run on the same date.

-   Step 6 — Summarize: Write one row to etf_cycle_summary with
    aggregate cycle metadata.

-   Step 7 — Return: Include qualifying ETFs in the hot_etfs array of
    the API response.
