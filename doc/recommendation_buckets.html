Recommendation Engine: The 3-Bucket Strategy

MoneyGoUp's recommendation engine classifies stocks into three distinct buckets to provide tailored BUY/SELL signals. This system ensures that user-owned positions, monitored interests, and AI-discovered opportunities are treated with the appropriate level of urgency and validation.

📊 Bucket 1: Portfolio

Positions that the user has officially purchased and confirmed. These represent real financial stakes and require the highest precision for signals.

  • Signals: Surfaces both BUY (add to position) and SELL (exit/trim) alerts.
  • Logic: Compares the AI 1-month prediction against the current live market price.
is_purchased = 1 AND user_confirmed = 1 AND shares > 0 AND is_active = 1
Positive: +3% (default)
Negative: -3% (default)
👀 Bucket 2: Watchlist

Stocks that the user is actively monitoring but does not yet own. These items have been explicitly "confirmed" by the user (e.g., by clicking "Add to Watchlist").

  • Signals: Surfaces BUY alerts only.
  • Logic: Monitors for significant upward breakouts predicted by the AI.
is_purchased = 0 AND user_confirmed = 1 AND is_active = 1
Threshold: +5% (default)
✨ Bucket 3: Discovery

High-potential stocks identified automatically by the deepmoney_sync.py engine. These appear as "unconfirmed" and do not show up in the "My Watchlist" section until the user interacts with them.

  • Discovery Logic: Stocks qualify for this bucket if they meet the following sync thresholds:
    • GPS Score > 6
    • Predicted Change % > 5%
  • Target Users: Qualifying stocks are added for every user who has logged in within the past 7 days.
  • Signals: Surfaces BUY alerts only.
is_purchased = 0 AND user_confirmed = 0 AND is_active = 1
Threshold: +5% (default)

Environment Configuration

The signal thresholds for these buckets are fully configurable via .env.local:

  • RECOMMENDATION_PORTFOLIO_POSITIVE_THRESHOLD: Target % for portfolio BUY signals.
  • RECOMMENDATION_PORTFOLIO_NEGATIVE_THRESHOLD: Target % for portfolio SELL signals.
  • RECOMMENDATION_WATCHLIST_THRESHOLD: Target % for both Watchlist and Discovery BUY signals.
